# Codex brief — runtime 0.45.0 (2026-09-29)

Tracks Cachalot runtime **0.45.0** (HANDOFF section 18.25). One new environment knob and one new log line; no
endpoint changed.

- **New knob (MiniMax server and chat):** `CACHALOT_MINIMAX_MIRROR_ADAPT` (default 1). With the X10Pro mirror
  mounted, the share of each expert read served by the mirror follows both drives' measured speed, between 2 % and
  `CACHALOT_MIRROR_FRACTION` (0.13). 0 keeps the share fixed at `CACHALOT_MIRROR_FRACTION`. Belongs next to the
  mirror settings in Studio's launch panel (advanced), default shown.
- **New log line:** `[bank] mirror share 0.13 -> 0.09 (configured 0.13)`, at most once a minute and only when the
  share moved by 0.03 or more. A Cockpit storage panel may show the latest value as "mirror share" beside the
  mirror's mount state; a share well under the configured value means the X10Pro is busy with something else.
- **Advice Studio may surface:** when the share sits low for minutes, suggest excluding the X10Pro's `models` folder
  from Spotlight (System Settings → Spotlight → Search Privacy).
